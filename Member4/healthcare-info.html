Healthcare Information

What you should know before donating

Eligibility basics, what to expect, and answers to common questions about blood donation.

Eligibility basics

Age & weight

18–65 years

Donors should generally weigh at least 50 kg and be in good general health.

Frequency

Every 3 months

Whole blood donation is usually safe to repeat once every 90 days.

Before donating

Eat & hydrate

Have a proper meal and drink water a few hours before your appointment.

After donating

Rest 10–15 min

Avoid heavy exercise and stay hydrated for the rest of the day.

Frequently asked questions

Most donors feel only a brief pinch when the needle goes in. The donation itself is usually painless and takes about 10–15 minutes.
A standard donation collects around 350–450 ml, which is a small fraction of the body's total blood volume and is safely replenished within days.
People who are unwell, underweight, pregnant, or recently had major surgery or certain infections should wait or consult a doctor first.
Most guidelines recommend waiting at least 6 months after a tattoo or piercing before donating.
